SAMURAI BONK'S ADVENTURE: ROAD TO THE CASTLE
ZUTSUKI! Godot 4 Windows prototype, three connected playable levels.

IMPORT: Open Godot 4 Standard, Import project.godot, press F5.
CONTROLS: WASD/Arrows movement, Space headbutt, Shift dash, F1 developer menu, R restart.

LEVEL 1 (0-160m): Bamboo Downhill. Three active mystery doors in each wall. Correct gate glows.
LEVEL 2 (160-320m): Guard Gauntlet. Five mystery doors per wall; moving giant guards.
LEVEL 3 (320-477m): Castle Chaos. More guards, secret side walls, golden kabuto finish.

FEATURES: Bonk combos and score, mystery doors, guard weak spot, 20m secret shortcuts, Kabuto victory confetti, Waradaruma, saved developer settings and best time.
DEFAULTS: speed 12, camera 20, Daruma 1, leaves 85, door glow on.

IMPORTANT: This is a procedural Godot prototype. Samurai is currently a 2D image billboard. Full 3D rigged models, sound, and detailed scenery require separate assets and implementation. Not fully playtested in Godot.
